Share app audio

Takes the sound from one app on your PC, like Spotify, a browser or a game, and plays it through your microphone. People in your Discord call or game chat hear it as if it were coming from your mic, mixed together with your voice.

Only the app you pick is shared. Notifications, other apps and your own call audio stay private.

Selected app➜MicMix➜CABLE mic➜Discord / game

Set its loudness with App volume and App gain in the bar at the bottom, and use Mute app to pause it for everyone without stopping the song.

Don't pick your call app itself (Discord, or the browser Discord is open in), or people will hear themselves echo.

AI voices

Don't have it yet? Download MicMix-AI.zip, then click Install and pick it. Don't unzip it.

AI voices turn your voice into a trained voice model. They use your graphics card only while one is picked and you're talking, so pick a normal preset when you're gaming.
